## The correspondence

The statements are relative to the pinned draft.  Proposition 4.30 reads:

> There is a template system `L` such that
> - all templates in `L` are finite legal templates, and
> - all finite legal templates are isomorphic to renamings of templates in `L`.

A template system is Definition 4.11: a collection `T` of templates with the clauses (a)–(g), and
`T* ⊆ T` with the four clauses on `T*` (`T* ⊆ T`; points and cells initial segments of `ω`; every
`t ∈ T` on `[n]` has a renaming in `T*`; renamings within `T*` are equal).  Legal templates are
Definition 4.29; renaming is Definition 4.6.  These four statements are among those audited against [AFK26] v1.  The notions
also used below — plans (Definition 4.1), isomorphism of templates (Definition 4.7), restriction
and extension (Definition 4.8) and truncation (Definition 4.10) — are not among the audited
statements; for them the correspondence is to the pinned draft.

**Positional reading.**  A template on `[n]` is a stage type `t : StageType ω₁ n`: points `Fin n`,
cells `Fin t.card`, labels in `{-∞} ∪ ω₁ ∪ {∞}`.  A legal template is `t.IsLegalTemplate ω₁`, whose
fields are the five printed clauses of Definition 4.29 (with the label range of Definition 4.4).
The restriction `t↾A₀` to a closed set is a defined face `StageType.restrictFace f t = some s` along
a point embedding `f : Fin m ↪ Fin n` ("`u` extends `s`" is `StageType.restrictFace f u = some s`;
the face is defined exactly at closed sets of points); `trunc_λ` at a block stage
`λ = ω + ω · β` (`blockStage β`) is `StageType.project (isSuccPrelimit_blockStage β)`;
`trunc_δ(v) ≅_ren trunc_δ(u)` at an arbitrary ordinal `δ` is `StageType.IsTruncRenaming δ v u`, on
the raw truncated labellings (no truncated stage type is formed); `t₀ ≅_ren t₁` is
`StageType.IsCellRenaming t₀ t₁`.  Printed templates live on nonempty sets, so the arity is
positive throughout (`0 < n`, explicit in `LegalTemplates` and `chosenLegalTemplates`); natively a
legal class on zero points exists, and it is excluded.

## The positional statement

Namespace `VaughtConjecture`, file
`VaughtConjecture/Correspondence/TemplateSystem/LegalSystem.lean`.  The family `T` of the printed
proof and the system `L`, read positionally:

```lean

noncomputable def chosenTemplateRepresentative {n : ℕ} (c : TemplateClass.{u} ω₁ n) :
```

`TemplateSystemClauses T` is a structure with one field per clause (b)–(g) of Definition 4.11;
clause (a) (points and cells finite subsets of `ω`) holds by typing, the points being `Fin n` and

```lean
def ReindexClause : Prop :=
  ∀ ⦃m n : ℕ⦄ (e : Fin m ≃ Fin n) ⦃t : StageType.{u} ω₁ n⦄, t ∈ T n → t.reindex e ∈ T m

def FaceClause : Prop :=
  ∀ ⦃m n : ℕ⦄ (f : Fin m ↪ Fin n) ⦃t : StageType.{u} ω₁ n⦄ ⦃s : StageType.{u} ω₁ m⦄, 0 < m →

def ProjectClause : Prop :=

def ProjectNeClause : Prop :=
```

equation itself states that `range f` is closed in `u'`.

## The full-carrier statements

File `VaughtConjecture/Correspondence/TemplateSystem/LegalCarrier.lean` (the Props are stated in
`LegalSystem.lean`).  "Isomorphic to a renaming" ([AFK26, Definitions 4.6 and 4.7]) is the
repository's `IsIsoRenaming j D R p t`: there is a cell bijection `e : ι ≃ Fin t.card` along which,
with the point embedding `j : Fin n ↪ P`, the underlying set of `(D, R, p)` is the image of `[n]`,
its plan is the image of the plan of `t`, and scopes, grades, labels and rows correspond
(`IsIsoRenamingAlong`, fields `ground_eq`, `faces_eq`, `scope_eq`, `grade_eq`, `label_eq`,
`row_eq`).

The second bullet on a finite type of points:

```lean
def FintypeCarrierClause (L : ∀ n, Set (StageType.{u} ω₁ n)) : Prop :=
  ∀ (P : Type w) [Fintype P] [DecidableEq P] (n : ℕ) (i : P ≃ Fin n) {ι : Type w} [Finite ι]
    (D : CellScheme ι P) (R : D.Rows.{u}) (p : ι → Label.{u}), D.ground = Finset.univ →
    Geometry.IsTemplatePlan D.ground D.faces → R.IsLegalTemplate ω₁ p →
    ∃ t ∈ L n, IsIsoRenaming i.symm.toEmbedding D R p t

def Proposition430FintypeTarget : Prop :=
  Proposition430Target.{u} ∧ FintypeCarrierClause.{u, w} chosenLegalTemplates.{u}

theorem proposition430FintypeTarget : Proposition430FintypeTarget.{u, w}
```

The second bullet for the printed collection `T`, underlying sets and cells finite subsets of `ℕ`
(Definition 4.11 (a)):

```lean
def FinsetNatCarrierClause (L : ∀ n, Set (StageType.{u} ω₁ n)) : Prop :=
  ∀ (A C : Finset ℕ) (n : ℕ) (i : A ≃ Fin n) (D : CellScheme C ℕ) (R : D.Rows.{u})
    (p : C → Label.{u}), D.ground = A → Geometry.IsTemplatePlan D.ground D.faces →

  Proposition430Target.{u} ∧ FinsetNatCarrierClause.{u} chosenLegalTemplates.{u}
```

Each full-carrier form is the positional statement together with the second bullet for legal
templates whose points are not `Fin n`: every legal template ([AFK26, Definitions 4.1 and 4.29],
`CellScheme.Rows.IsLegalTemplate ω₁`) with finitely many cells, whose underlying set is all of a
finite type `P` (respectively a finite set `A ⊆ ℕ` with cells a finite set `C ⊆ ℕ`), is isomorphic,
along the given point bijection `i⁻¹`, to a renaming of a member of `chosenLegalTemplates n`.
Hypotheses, explicitly: in the `Fintype` form, `[Fintype P]`, `[DecidableEq P]`, an explicit
equivalence `i : P ≃ Fin n`, `[Finite ι]` for the cells, the underlying set all of `P`
(`D.ground = Finset.univ`), a plan (`Geometry.IsTemplatePlan`, nonempty by Definition 4.1, so
`0 < n` is derived, not assumed) and legality at `ω₁`; points and cells at one universe `w`, labels
at `u`.  In the `ℕ` form, `A C : Finset ℕ`, an explicit `i : A ≃ Fin n`, `D.ground = A`, a plan and
legality at `ω₁`; points and cells at universe `0`, labels at `u`.  No countability premise.

## How the statements are proved

Clauses (b)–(d) are closure of the strongly coded legal stage types under reindexing, defined
faces and truncation, through the identification of the legal templates of Definition 4.29 with
the strongly coded legal stage types at `ω₁` (`StageType.isLegalTemplate_omega_one_iff_of_le`,
from `StageType.isLegalTemplate_iff`, proved before this milestone).  Clause (e) is the chosen
representative of a legal class on one point not fixed by `τ_β` (`exists_legalClass_project_ne`);
(f) is a legal class on every positive number of points (`exists_legalClass`).  Clause (g) follows
the printed proof: a cap `c` with `δ < c < λ` self-visible at `n`
(`Label.exists_lt_lt_isSelfVisible`); bountifulness of the rows of `u'` from the face along `f` to
all points, at the cap (`StageType.exists_isLawful_lift`); the stage type `v` on the scheme of `u'`
with the lifted labels (`StageType.ofIsLawful`), whose face along `f` is `t`
(`StageType.restrictFace_ofIsLawful`); agreement with `u'` capped at `c`, hence after `trunc_δ`
(`Label.min_eq_min_iff_reduce_eq`).  The witness `v` keeps the frame and rows of `u'` and changes
only the labels, and `v' = v`.  Clauses (b)–(g) hold for `LegalTemplates`.  Separately, the chosen
representatives satisfy `IsTemplateSystemOver LegalTemplates chosenLegalTemplates`, using
`Quotient.out_eq` and `StageType.toClass_eq_toClass_iff`.  The full-carrier forms pull a legal
template back along `i⁻¹` to the points `Fin n` (legality along a point embedding,
`CellScheme.Rows.IsLegalTemplate.comap`), number its cells (`CellScheme.Rows.toStageTypeOfLegal`),
take the chosen representative of its class, and compose the isomorphisms
(`isIsoRenamingAlong_toStageType`, `IsIsoRenamingAlong.of_comap`,
`IsIsoRenamingAlong.renameCells`); the `ℕ` form is the `Fintype` form for the points of `A`, after
pulling back along the inclusion of `A` into `ℕ`.
