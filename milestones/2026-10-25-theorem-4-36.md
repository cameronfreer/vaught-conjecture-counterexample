## The statement

Namespace `VaughtConjecture`, files `VaughtConjecture/Correspondence/TemplateSystem/*.lean`.  The
sentence `templateSentence : baseTemplateLanguage.Sentenceω` is the transcription of
`σ[L]` ([AFK26, Definition 4.22]; `Correspondence/Sentence/TemplateSentence.lean`); the spectrum
notion is the repository's `MainTheorem.HasThinAlephOneSpectrum` (`MainTheorem/Spectrum.lean`),
for models coded on `ℕ`.

```lean
def Theorem436Target : Prop :=
  MainTheorem.HasThinAlephOneSpectrum templateSentence.{0}

theorem theorem436Target : Theorem436Target
```

The models of `templateSentence` coded on `ℕ` have exactly `ℵ₁` isomorphism classes, and there is
no perfect set of pairwise non-isomorphic such models.  The two halves are separate theorems:

```lean
def TemplateSentenceAlephOneTarget : Prop :=
  Cardinal.mk (Quotient (isoSetoid templateSentence.{0})) = Cardinal.aleph 1
theorem templateSentenceAlephOneTarget : TemplateSentenceAlephOneTarget

def ThinnessTarget : Prop :=
  ¬ templateSentence.{0}.HasPerfectSetOfPairwiseNonisomorphicNatModels
theorem thinnessTarget : ThinnessTarget
```

Exactly `ℵ₁` (`TemplateSystem/AlephOne.lean`): the stage of the unique maximal lift
(`sentenceMaximalLiftTarget`, Claim 4.37) is a function `maximalStageOfClass` on the isomorphism
classes, defined using the greatest lift stage rather than the least projection-fixing stage (no
equality or inequality between the two notions is asserted or used); its fibres are countable
(condition (b) below with `baseReductEquiv`), its range is unbounded below `ω₁`, and
`Counting.mk_eq_aleph_one_of_rank` gives the count.  Thinness (`TemplateSystem/BackAndForth.lean`):
[AFK26, Proposition 2.18] for `K[L]` as a back-and-forth system on the base reducts of members of
`K_η` with matching invariant tuples (`IsMatchedAt`, `backAndForthCoreTarget`, by induction on the
level with atomic agreement, forth and back steps and monotonicity), its passage to the empty
tuple (`backAndForthEmptyTupleTarget`), countably many `bfEquivSetoid templateSentence β` classes
at each `β < ω₁` ([AFK26, Theorem 2.19]; `countableBFClassesTarget`), and
`MainTheorem.isThinOnNatModels_of_countable_bfClasses`.

## The system behind the theorem

`TemplateSystem/Conditions.lean`: the four conditions of [AFK26, Definition 2.23] for the
template system of the strongly coded legal templates, `templateSystemConditionsTarget`:

```lean
def TemplateSystemConditionsTarget : Prop :=
  (∀ M : Type v, MaximalCoverageTarget.{0} M) ∧ UniformMaximalRepresentativesTarget.{v} ∧
    (∀ M : Type v, AttainedEventualDeterminationTarget.{0} M) ∧
    ∀ M : Type v, InvariantOccurrenceTarget.{0, v, 0} M
```

(a) maximal coverage from Claim 4.37 (`maximalCompletionTarget`); (b) for each `β < ω₁` one
countable family of maximal representatives on `ℕ`, chosen before the source carriers
(`maximalCountTarget`, Proposition 4.35) — no globally countable family is claimed; (c) attained
eventual determination (`eventualDeterminationTarget`, Definition 2.21); (d) invariant occurrence
in the universal chart model (`universalChartModelTarget`, Lemma 4.33).
`TemplateSystem/WeakSystem.lean`: the invariant system of [AFK26, Definitions 2.6 and 4.16] on the
template signature with every field (`templateInvariantSystem`: countably many invariants fixed at
each level, nonstationarity, a fixing level for every invariant), its compatibility with the
template diagram ([AFK26, Definition 2.8, Lemma 4.19]), the identification of its structure
projection with `projectStructure` (`templateInvariantSystem_projection`, Definition 2.7),
projected density from scalar density (`projectedDensityAt_of_satisfiesDensityAt`,
Definition 2.10 (c)), and the weak-system clauses (`weakSystemClausesTarget`, Definition 2.17,
Theorem 4.23); `templateBaseModelsTarget`, the base of the system is the class of models of
`templateSentence` on countable nonempty carriers.  `baseLanguageCountableTarget`: the base
template language has countably many symbols.

Hypotheses, explicitly: the models counted are those coded on `ℕ` (`isoSetoid templateSentence`,
`ModelsOf`); the ordinal and the language are at universe `0`; the carriers of the four conditions
are countable, and nonempty by membership in the classes `K_β`.  Every input is a compiled theorem
of this repository; there are no unproved theorem hypotheses.

## What is not claimed, at the pinned commit

The inventory below describes the pinned commit, not the current state of the repository.

- The theorem concerns `templateSentence`, the transcription of `σ[L]`, separately from and
  without reference to the repository's main theorem for its own sentence
  `baseLanguage.densitySentence` (`MainTheorem.densitySentence_hasThinAlephOneSpectrum_levels`).
  No identification of the two sentences is made, and no spectrum result is transferred in either
  direction.
- The statement is about models coded on `ℕ`; a spectrum statement on all countable carriers
  is not made here.  This is a distinction of scope, not an obstruction: the empty structure also
  satisfies the sentence, so an all-carriers formulation would count one further class, and the
  passage from models on countable nonempty carriers to models coded on `ℕ` is proved
  (`nonemptyCarrierPassageTarget`, `templateModelInfiniteTarget`).
- This is the concrete specialization of the route of Section 2 of [AFK26] to the strongly coded
  template system.  The abstract theorems of that section for arbitrary qualifying systems
  (Theorems 2.25 and 2.27) are not stated.
- The projection theorem `projectionTarget` still carries `Countable M`; Proposition 4.30 (the
  template-system clauses of Definition 4.11 for the legal templates) is not yet packaged; both
  are recorded as later work.
- Verification applies to the pinned commit.  Comparator checks the repository's reference
  statement (the main theorem through `comparator/Challenge.lean`); it does not separately
  check the manuscript dictionary or each new theorem.  Those are checked by the build and the
  axiom audit.
