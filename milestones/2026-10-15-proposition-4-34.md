## The statements

Namespace `VaughtConjecture`, file `VaughtConjecture/Correspondence/Proposition434.lean`.  The
class `K_β` is `templateStageClass M β` (`VaughtConjecture/Correspondence/Density/Density.lean`),
the base reduct `N↾L_c[L]` is `baseReduct`, the projection `τ_β` is `projectStructure β`, and
the rank of a lift is the least projection-fixing stage, `IsLeast (fixingStages G) ρ`
(`VaughtConjecture/Correspondence/Density/Language.lean`,
`VaughtConjecture/Correspondence/Boundedness/Rank.lean`):

```lean
def liftRanks (B : baseTemplateLanguage.{u}.Structure M) : Set Ordinal.{u} :=
  {ρ | ∃ β < ω₁, ∃ G ∈ templateStageClass M β, baseReduct G = B ∧ IsLeast (fixingStages G) ρ}
```

The three printed clauses:

```lean
structure Proposition434Clauses (β : Ordinal.{u}) (M : Type v)
    (B : baseTemplateLanguage.{u}.Structure M) : Prop where
  boundedness : (liftRanks M B).Countable ∧ ∃ A < ω₁, ∀ ρ ∈ liftRanks M B, ρ < A
  reconstruction : ∀ G ∈ templateStageClass M β, ∀ H ∈ templateStageClass M β,
    baseReduct G = B → baseReduct H = B → G = H
  limits : Order.IsSuccLimit β → (∀ ξ < β, ∃ G ∈ templateStageClass M ξ, baseReduct G = B) →
    ∃ G ∈ templateStageClass M β, baseReduct G = B
```

They hold for every base structure on a countable carrier and every `β < ω₁`, with no sentence
premise:

```lean
theorem proposition434Clauses_of_countable {M : Type v} (hc : Countable M) {β : Ordinal.{u}}
    (hβ : β < ω₁) (B : baseTemplateLanguage.{u}.Structure M) : Proposition434Clauses β M B
```

The sentence-facing form, with the printed premises; its `Nonempty M` and `σ[L]` premises are
not used, which is where the printed premise `M ⊨ σ[L]` is dropped:

```lean
def Proposition434Target (M : Type v) : Prop :=
  Countable M → Nonempty M → ∀ β : Ordinal.{u}, β < ω₁ →
    ∀ B : baseTemplateLanguage.{u}.Structure M, @Sentenceω.Realize _ templateSentence.{u} M B →
      Proposition434Clauses β M B

theorem proposition434Target (M : Type v) : Proposition434Target.{u, v} M
```

Level zero is a separate theorem, which does need the sentence and a nonempty carrier:

```lean
theorem exists_lift_zero_of_realize_templateSentence {M : Type v} [Nonempty M]
    (B : baseTemplateLanguage.{u}.Structure M) (h : @Sentenceω.Realize _ templateSentence.{u} M B) :
    ∃ G ∈ templateStageClass M (0 : Ordinal.{u}), baseReduct G = B
```

Hypotheses, explicitly: countable carriers; `β < ω₁`; for the Limits clause, `β` a nonzero
limit (`Order.IsSuccLimit` excludes zero, the manuscript's convention), with the zero level
handled separately through the sentence correspondence; for the zero level, a nonempty carrier
and the sentence.  Rank means the least projection-fixing stage, not Scott rank; only rank at
most stage is used, and no rank-equals-stage claim is made.

## What is not claimed, at the pinned commit

The inventory below describes the pinned commit, not the current state of the repository.

- The sentence `templateSentence` is not identified with the repository's public sentence
  `baseLanguage.densitySentence`; no spectrum result is transferred between them.  The separation
  theorem `classStructureSeparationTarget` (`Correspondence/Sentence/Dictionary.lean`) is a
  statement about one specified interpretation, the class reading, and is not a component of
  Proposition 4.34.
- Proposition 4.35 (countably many maximal structures at each level) is open at the pinned
  commit.  Its inputs under construction there are the restricted residual and hollow receiving
  properties of the strongly coded class, printed maximality and its relation to terminality,
  transport and infinitude; the native terminal counting at the pinned commit is conditional on
  those inputs.
- The language of `L°`, the sentence `σ[L°]` and the dictionary items D4–D7 and the rest of D9
  are deferred; D1, D2 and the forward D3 are stated.
- Verification applies to the pinned commit.  Comparator checks the repository's reference
  statement (the main theorem through `comparator/Challenge.lean`); it does not independently
  check the manuscript dictionary or each new theorem.  Those are checked by the build and the
  axiom audit.
- The proof of Proposition 4.34 does not depend on the open work toward Proposition 4.35.
