## The correspondence

The statements are relative to the pinned draft.  They are not an assertion that the pinned
draft equals the published version of the paper, nor that the draft's text is final.  Maximality
at a level is Definition 2.20 of the pinned draft, transcribed literally with its allowance for
an unchanged higher lift; the classes `K_β` are Definition 4.21; Proposition 4.35 is the
countability, up to isomorphism, of the `(L_b[L] ∪ L_c[L])`-structures maximal at a level.

## The statements

Namespace `VaughtConjecture`, files `VaughtConjecture/Correspondence/Maximality/Definitions.lean`
and `VaughtConjecture/Correspondence/Maximality/Count.lean`.  The class `K_β` is
`templateStageClass M β`, the projection `τ_β` is `projectStructure β`, and the language of the
structures is `templateLanguage`, the template language of the dictionary
(`VaughtConjecture/Correspondence/Density/Language.lean`).

```lean
def IsMaximalAt (β : Ordinal.{u}) (G : templateLanguage.{u}.Structure M) : Prop :=
  G ∈ templateStageClass M β ∧
    ∀ β' : Ordinal.{u}, β' < ω₁ → β < β' → ∀ G' ∈ templateStageClass M β',
      projectStructure β G' = G → G = G'
```

The headline statement, with the manuscript's convention of nonempty domains:

```lean
def Proposition435Target (β : Ordinal.{0}) : Prop :=
  β < ω₁ → ∃ S : Set (templateLanguage.{0}.Structure ℕ), S.Countable ∧
    (∀ H ∈ S, IsMaximalAt β H) ∧
    ∀ (M : Type v) [Countable M] [Nonempty M] (G : templateLanguage.{0}.Structure M),
      IsMaximalAt β G → ∃ H ∈ S, Nonempty (@Language.Equiv templateLanguage.{0} M ℕ G H)

theorem proposition435Target (β : Ordinal.{0}) : Proposition435Target β
```

The stronger form, without the nonemptiness premise, from which it is derived:

```lean
def MaximalCountTarget (β : Ordinal.{0}) : Prop :=
  β < ω₁ → ∃ S : Set (templateLanguage.{0}.Structure ℕ), S.Countable ∧
    (∀ H ∈ S, IsMaximalAt β H) ∧
    ∀ (M : Type v) [Countable M] (G : templateLanguage.{0}.Structure M), IsMaximalAt β G →
      ∃ H ∈ S, Nonempty (@Language.Equiv templateLanguage.{0} M ℕ G H)

theorem maximalCountTarget (β : Ordinal.{0}) : MaximalCountTarget β
```

For every level `β < ω₁`: a countable set of structures on `ℕ`, each maximal at `β`, such that
every structure maximal at `β` on a countable carrier is isomorphic to a member of the set.  The
isomorphism is in the full template language `templateLanguage`, not merely of the base reducts.
Hypotheses, explicitly: `β < ω₁`; countable carriers (and nonempty carriers in the headline
form); the ordinal and the language are at universe `0`, the carrier at any universe.

## The native count, distinct from Proposition 4.35

`VaughtConjecture/MainTheorem/StronglyCodedTerminalCount.lean`:

```lean
theorem Realization.stronglyCodedTerminalClasses_countable {ξ : Ordinal.{0}} (hξ : ξ < ω₁) :
    (stronglyCodedTerminalClasses ξ).Countable
```

This is the countability of the isomorphism classes of strongly coded terminal realizations on
`ℕ` (realizations in the strongly coded receiving class, `Realization.IsStronglyCodedReceiving`,
with no successor in that class at the next block, `Realization.IsStronglyCodedTerminalAt`), with
the restricted residual and hollow receiving properties of that class proved
(`Realization.stronglyCodedResidualReceiving`, `Realization.stronglyCodedHollowReceiving`) and
no premise beyond the stage bound.  It is a statement about the repository's realizations, not
about the manuscript's maximal structures.  Proposition 4.35 is obtained from it through the
presentations of the members of `K_β` by strongly coded receiving realizations
(`stronglyCodedPresentationTarget`), maximality implying terminality of the presentation
(`maximalToTerminalTarget`, by strictness at the successor level `successorStrictnessTarget`),
infinitude of the carrier (`infinitudeTarget`), transport along bijections of carriers, and the
passage from an isomorphism of realizations to an isomorphism of class-reading structures
(`Realization.Iso.classReadingEquiv`).  The representatives are chosen only among the terminal
classes presented by some maximal structure on `ℕ`.

## What is not claimed, at the pinned commit

The inventory below describes the pinned commit, not the current state of the repository.

- The structures counted are those members of `K_β` that are maximal at `β`, up to full
  template-language isomorphism.  No identification of them with the models of the repository's
  public sentence `baseLanguage.densitySentence` is made, and no spectrum result is transferred
  between the two.
- The countability of the maximal structures at a level does not assert that maximal structures
  exist at every level.
- The converse of `maximalToTerminalTarget` (that a strongly coded terminal presentation comes
  from a maximal structure) is not stated.
- Uniqueness of maximal lifts across levels and the existence of a greatest level of a lift
  (the statements C.T3 and C.T4 of the plan) are not stated; they are not used.
- General cross-level strictness on arbitrary carriers is not stated: strictness at the successor
  level needs no countability, and the general form is proved on countable carriers only.
- Verification applies to the pinned commit.  Comparator checks the repository's reference
  statement (the main theorem through `comparator/Challenge.lean`); it does not independently
  check the manuscript dictionary or each new theorem.  Those are checked by the build and the
  axiom audit.
