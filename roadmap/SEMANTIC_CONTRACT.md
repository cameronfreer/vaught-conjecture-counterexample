# Semantic contract

The meanings that any reformulation or independent formalization must preserve.
The mathematical object is fixed by the construction itself, not by the shortened prose.

1. **Logic and spectrum.** The language is relational, with one relation for each base-stage finite type. The sentence of the main theorem is in `L_{ω₁,ω}` with actual library satisfaction. Preserve the distinction between models on `ℕ` and all countable carriers; use the proved absence of finite models to identify their spectra. Do not replace perfect-set thinness with a continuum inequality.

2. **Finite geometry.** Preserve the recursive visible-face plans, equivalently the proved convex geometries with exactly two extremes per nonsingleton closed set. Not every convex geometry is admitted. Two generators do not imply a two-element closed root, a bounded hull size, a linear interval representation, or convex dimension two in the literature's sense.

3. **Labels and rows.** Preserve cell scopes and grades, order/availability/locality, the visibility replacement maps, and the special bottom/top values. A cap is an observation and must satisfy the condition of its use: a permitted cutoff in receiving and density, a self-visible cap in bountifulness and lawful capping. Capping is not stage reduction. Capped vectors need not be lawful at an arbitrary cutoff; capping a lawful section at a cap self-visible at a bound on its grades is lawful [Kni26, Lemma 2.5.8], and bountifulness concerns exactly these self-visible caps, not the permitted cutoffs of receiving (Layer 3 of `README.md`). Retain the cap conditions on auxiliary rows and future fields even when the final observation ignores them.

4. **Actual evaluation.** A tuple's partial type is exact under face maps. Absence of a face is mathematical information. Inside a finite partial master, a supported invisible tuple and a tuple not yet supported are different states. The final closure reformulation does not erase this construction-time distinction.

5. **Model families and the sentence.** Use the original nonempty, exact consistency, covering, general-family, bottom-pattern, uniformity, and high-arity-dominance clauses; the four families remain the required extension properties. The four families demand some suitable coface; do not strengthen them to exact realization of every prescribed coface. The sentence of the main theorem is the density sentence (the structural clauses, namely injective tuples, unique labels, exact face coherence, covering, and nonemptiness, and the one-point capped-extension clause); its equivalence with the sentence expressing the four families is a required theorem, not an optional companion. Conversely, do not weaken the actual finite extension constructions to mere catalogue membership (the catalogue and legality are defined in Layer 3 of `README.md`).

6. **Receiving.** Construct actual finite rows, then an actual occurrence over the literal root, then recover the required donor labels from that occurrence (the vocabulary is that of Layer 3 of `README.md`). Keep proper-value equality, literal-top equality, and above-threshold recovery of top separate. Arbitrary lawful ambient inputs and every allowed cap are part of the finite statement.

7. **Stable candidate.** Stable values are computed on actual transported cells in directed covers. Finite stable offsets decode to `α+n`; escape decodes to formal top, not `α+ω`. Prove structural lawfulness before modelhood. Any next-block model expansion normalizes to this candidate; cap receiving proves existence separately.

8. **Terminal countability.** A countable cover of the terminal classes at each countable stage by subsingletons suffices. Do not require the cover to be disjoint, a canonical least core, characteristic arity, or a global rank. The grade-zero case is handled by the empty rigid core. “Rigid core” is a top-support condition, not automorphism rigidity. “Hollow” is the original no-anchor predicate, with stable-label fixedness as a theorem for models.

9. **Domains.** `D_ξ` means expansion existence up to isomorphism, normalized to the same carrier when needed. Limit continuity requires the actual limit construction. Successor-loss countability and nonemptiness have different proofs. A terminal witness lies in a loss only after controlling alternative expansions. No global departure assumption is allowed in the preferred proof of the main theorem.

10. **Definability.** Explicit coordinate formulas currently prove uniqueness for actual charts of a specified type with the specified extreme coordinates. New claims about generated substructures or definable closure must prove the passage to arbitrary pairs and arbitrary sets. Automorphism-fixedness alone does not supply a formula. No reverse inclusion `dcl ⊆ cl` or equality with `acl` is assumed. Preservation of the hull operations by embeddings of realizations is proved from the charts that witness them, not inferred from their definability.

11. **Reconstruction of a classical limit.** An abstract Fraïssé limit of the age of top-free charts is not by itself a realization; it becomes the top-free witness only through a reconstruction of its partial evaluation that proves all of the following for the limit structure (`README.md`, the section on the top-free witnesses). *Literal recovery of chart relations:* the chart relation \(P_p\) holds at a tuple exactly when the tuple is injective and its reconstructed evaluation is \(p\). *Uniqueness and injectivity of labelled tuples:* at most one chart relation holds at a tuple, and a tuple at which one holds is injective. *Exact partial restriction:* the evaluation of a face of an evaluated tuple is the literal restriction of its type, and an invisible face returns `none`. *Covering:* every finite tuple, the empty tuple and tuples with repeated coordinates included, lies in the points of an actual occurrence. *Receiving with all equations attached to one occurrence:* over a literal root, the restriction to the root, the freshness of the new point (distinct from every point of the root, so outside the whole chart when the root is a whole actual occurrence), and the agreement with the donor below the permitted cutoff are equations about one and the same occurrence; for a donor containing top labels this holds at each permitted cutoff separately, with an extension depending on the cutoff. *The literal roundtrip, in both directions:* reconstructing from a canonical expanded realization returns that realization, and expanding a reconstructed, locally charted structure by the definable hull operations returns its original relations **and** its original functions. Local chart coverage (every finite tuple in a finitely generated substructure isomorphic to a chart) suffices for the identification of the functions; homogeneity is needed only later, for receiving. Receiving itself uses only the literal recovery of the chart relations. The regressions are part of this item: empty carriers where the language allows them, repeated inputs, and the default values where no chart witness exists.

Completion means proving these concrete conditions and then deriving the main theorem, not constructing an abstract structure whose fields already assert them.
