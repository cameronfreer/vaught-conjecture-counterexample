# Semantic contract for a refactor or independent rewrite

The mathematical object is fixed by the construction itself, not by the shortened prose.

1. **Logic and spectrum.** The language is relational, with one relation for each base-stage finite type. The endpoint sentence is in `L_{ω₁,ω}` with actual library satisfaction. Preserve the distinction between models on `ℕ` and all countable carriers; use the proved absence of finite models to identify their spectra. Do not replace perfect-set thinness with a continuum inequality.

2. **Finite geometry.** Preserve the recursive visible-face plans, equivalently the proved convex geometries with exactly two extremes per nonsingleton closed set. Not every convex geometry is admitted. Two generators do not imply a two-element closed root, a bounded hull size, a linear interval representation, or convex dimension two in the literature's sense.

3. **Labels and rows.** Preserve cell scopes and grades, order/availability/locality, the visibility replacement maps, and the special bottom/top values. A cap is an observation and must satisfy its permitted-cap condition. Capping is not stage reduction; capped vectors need not be lawful. Retain auxiliary-row and future-field cap obligations even when a final observer ignores them.

4. **Actual evaluation.** A tuple's partial type is exact under face maps. Absence of a face is mathematical information. Inside a finite partial master, a supported invisible tuple and a tuple not yet supported are different states. The final closure reformulation does not erase this construction-time distinction.

5. **Model families.** Use the original nonempty, exact consistency, covering, general-family, bottom-pattern, uniform-band, and high-grade-dominance requirements. The four families demand some suitable coface; do not strengthen them to exact realization of every prescribed coface. Conversely, do not weaken the actual finite suppliers to mere catalogue membership.

6. **Receiving.** Produce actual finite rows, then an actual occurrence over the literal root, then the required readback. Keep proper-value equality, literal-top equality, and above-threshold top readback separate. Arbitrary lawful ambient inputs and every allowed cap are part of the finite contract.

7. **Stable candidate.** Stable values are computed on actual transported cells in directed covers. Finite stable offsets decode to `α+n`; escape decodes to formal top, not `α+ω`. Prove structural lawfulness before modelhood. Any next-block model expansion normalizes to this candidate; cap receiving proves existence separately.

8. **Terminal countability.** A countable cover by at-most-one-class conditions suffices. Do not require disjoint descriptors, a canonical least core, characteristic arity, or a global rank. The grade-zero boundary is handled by the empty rigid core. “Rigid core” is a top-support condition, not automorphism rigidity. “Hollow” is the original no-anchor predicate, with stable-label fixedness as a theorem for models.

9. **Domains.** `D_ξ` means expansion existence up to isomorphism, normalized to the same carrier when needed. Limit continuity requires the actual limit construction. Successor-loss countability and nonemptiness have different proofs. A terminal witness lies in a loss only after controlling alternative expansions. No global departure assumption is allowed in the preferred endpoint.

10. **Definability.** Explicit coordinate formulas currently prove uniqueness for actual charts of a specified type with the specified extreme coordinates. New claims about generated substructures or definable closure must prove the passage to arbitrary pairs and arbitrary sets. Automorphism-fixedness alone does not supply a formula. No reverse inclusion `dcl ⊆ cl` or equality with `acl` is assumed.

Completion means proving these concrete conditions and then deriving the endpoint, not constructing an abstract record whose fields already assert them.
